*** MANIPAL CAMPUS LORE & WORLD DATA ***

[LOCATIONS]
1.  Tiger Circle (TC): The chaotic center of campus. Always busy, full of rickshaws and rushing students.
2.  Student Plaza (SP): The social hub. Great place to buy, sell, and meet people.
3.  MIT Food Court 1: The noisy hall of sustenance.
4.  MIT Food Court 2 (FC2): Quieter dining area.
5.  End Point: A scenic park at the edge of campus. Students come here to relax.
6.  Central Library: Free AC. A sanctuary from the heat.
7.  KMC Greens: The romantic PvP zone. Couples and rivals alike gather here.
8.  DeeTee (The Ruins): An underground legend. Mysterious and atmospheric.
9.  Venugopal Temple: A peaceful temple. Many students visit before exams.
10. Canara Mall: The capitalist dungeon. Everything costs more than it should.
11. Hostel Block 10: The party fortress. Loud music at all hours.
12. The Innovation Centre (IC): Where hackathons and tech events happen. High energy.
13. Marena Complex: Sports arena. The competitive heart of campus.
14. The TAPMI Hill: A treacherous climb to a rival institution.
15. Academic Block 1 (AB1): A maze of classrooms. Easy to get lost.
16. Academic Block 5 (AB5): A towering structure. Home to many labs.
17. Kamath Circle (KC): Legendary tea shops. The best chai in Manipal.
18. Malpe Beach: A gorgeous beach a short ride away. Students go here to decompress.
19. Kapu Lighthouse: Grants vision of the coast. A beautiful viewpoint.
20. Inox Manipal: The cinema dungeon. A welcome escape from academic life.
21. Dollops: Expensive but delicious food.
22. Pangala: The breakfast shrine. Opens early, fills up fast.
23. Coin Circle: It's literally named Coin Circle. A small roundabout junction.
